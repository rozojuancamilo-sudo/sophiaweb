About Our Bakery

Our story

North Star Bakery was founded over 30 years ago. Founded by Gustav Adolfson, a famous baker from Sweden, North Star Bakery became the first real bakery in Kongsberg. At that time, most people made their own bread, and did not know what they were missing. Gustav brought Swedish fine pastries and breads that forever changed the quality of bread in Kongsberg.

Our ingredients

We try to use fresh ingredients and buy from local suppliers when possible.

Our team

Today, Gustav Adolf grandchildren run the bakery, and they prepare fresh bread every day.
